About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture ideas, reminders, and bits of information as they come to you. Keep your notes close at hand so it is easier to find them when you need them.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page and write a thought in the note field.
  2. Select Add note to submit your note.
  3. Return to your notes whenever you need to review an idea or reminder.

Features

  • A straightforward space for quick notes and reminders.
  • A simple interface that keeps note-taking focused.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common note-taking actions.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Enter Submit the current note
Ctrl + / Focus the note field
